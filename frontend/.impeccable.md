## Design Context

### Users
- **Target Audience**: Professional developers and engineering leads.
- **Context**: High-end productivity workspace that feels like a premium brand magazine.
- **Experience**: Institutional reliability meets editorial elegance. Focus as a prestigious "craft".

### Brand Personality
- **Voice & Tone**: Confident, institutional, yet softly editorial. 
- **Personality**: "The Global Registry" — prestigious, stable, and meticulously curated.
- **3 Words**: Prestigious, Circular, Editorial.

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Visual Tone**: **Mastercard Editorial** — warm cream surfaces, oversized radii, and orbital motifs.
- **Theme**: 
  - **Primary**: Canvas Cream (#F3F0EE) canvas with Ink Black (#141413) content.
  - **Accent**: Signal Orange (#CF4500) for cues, Light Signal Orange (#F37338) for connective arcs.
- **Structural Elements**: 
  - **Extreme Radius**: Stadium shapes (40px), pills (999px), and perfect circles. Zero sharp corners.
  - **Orbital Trajectory**: Connectivity implied by thin orange arcs and satellite circular CTAs.
- **Motion**: Fluid, circular transitions. Page elements feel like they are in "orbit".

### Design Principles
1. **Institutional Warmth**: Use Canvas Cream (#F3F0EE) exclusively as the foundation. Never sterile white.
2. **The Pill Constraint**: Every container must be a stadium, pill, or circle. Square is forbidden.
3. **Editorial Density**: Headlines must use -2% tracking and medium weights (500). Body copy at 450 weight.
4. **Connected Constellation**: Elements should feel linked by invisible orbital paths (thin arcs).
5. **Cushioned Elevation**: Shadows must be massive, soft, and low-opacity (spread 48px, opacity 8%).
